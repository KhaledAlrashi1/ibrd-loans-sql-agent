Assumptions

  • “Most undisbursed” ranks countries by total undisbursed amount (undisbursed_amount_usd), not by number of loans.
  • Guarantees excluded (is_guarantee = FALSE).
  • Regional entries (Caribbean, Eastern and Southern Africa, Southern Africa, Western and Central Africa) excluded from the country ranking.
  • undisbursed_amount_usd is in US$ at End of Period exchange rates (snapshot 2026-09-30).
  • All loan statuses included; amounts are summed across all of a country’s loans.
SQL 
SELECT country, SUM(undisbursed_amount_usd) AS undisbursed_usd
FROM ibrd_loans
WHERE is_guarantee = FALSE AND is_regional_entry = FALSE
GROUP BY country
ORDER BY undisbursed_usd DESC
LIMIT 10
