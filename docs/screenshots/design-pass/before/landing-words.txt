WHAT’S IN THIS DATA
One row
One IBRD loan or guarantee, by loan number. 9,538 rows, a snapshot as of Sep 30, 2026.
Main fields
Country and region, loan type and status, project, board approval date and fiscal year, and amounts in US$: approved, cancelled, undisbursed, disbursed, repaid and still owed.
Answers well
Totals, counts and rankings by country, region, fiscal year, loan status or loan type.
Can’t answer
Sectors, project results, GDP or other economic indicators, lending by IDA or anyone other than the IBRD, the currency of each loan (empty in this snapshot), anything after Sep 30, 2026, or forecasts.
Defaults
Loan questions leave out guarantees. “Year” means the World Bank fiscal year (July to June). The exchange-rate basis differs by amount column: undisbursed amounts are at end-of-period rates, disbursed and repaid amounts at historical rates.

Independent project, not affiliated with the World Bank.

Data: World Bank, IBRD Statement of Loans and Guarantees (DS00047), CC BY 4.0. Snapshot of Sep 30, 2026; columns renamed and two derived flags added.
